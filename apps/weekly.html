The verdict

Harkin, Webb, Chang, Prestwich, Conner, Kellar, Benn & Sheeran (2016), Psychological Bulletin 142(2):198–229. Meta-analysis of 138 studies (19,951 participants): prompting people to monitor goal progress had a small-to-medium effect on actually attaining the goal — and the effect was larger when progress was recorded rather than merely noticed. That's the whole justification for this page existing.

Three wins

Anything that went right, however small. You will systematically under-count these — the negatives are louder — so write them down before you look at what went wrong.

The bottleneck

Not everything that went wrong. The one thing that, if fixed, would have made the biggest difference to the numbers above.

One change only. If you change five things and next week is better, you've learned nothing about which one mattered. This is the difference between a system that improves and a system you keep rebuilding.
