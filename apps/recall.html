Why this works

The two strongest findings in learning science

1 · Testing effect. Retrieval isn't a measurement of memory, it changes memory. In Roediger & Karpicke's study, one study pass plus repeated recall beat four study passes on a one-week delayed test — even though the reread group predicted they'd do better.
Roediger & Karpicke (2006), Psychological Science 17(3):249–255.
2 · Spacing effect. Across 254 studies and 14,000+ participants, spreading the same total study time across separate sessions beat massing it, with the advantage growing as the retention interval got longer.
Cepeda, Pashler, Vul, Wixted & Rohrer (2006), Psychological Bulletin 132(3):354–380.
A large review for the US federal education agency rated practice testing and distributed practice as the only two techniques with high utility. Highlighting, rereading and summarising all scored low.
Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan & Willingham (2013), Psychological Science in the Public Interest 14(1):4–58.
