Step 1 of 6 · Score

Did this morning's plan actually happen?

Answer fast and honestly. This isn't a verdict on you — it's a signal about whether your plans are realistic, which is the only thing that will let you fix them.

Step 2 of 6 · Three good things

Three things that went well, and why.

The “why” is the part that does the work — it forces you to identify causes, some of which are things you did and can do again. Keep them small. “The coffee was good” is a valid entry.

Seligman, Steen, Park & Peterson (2005), American Psychologist 60(5):410–421. In a randomised placebo-controlled trial, participants who wrote three things that went well and why, every night for one week, were happier and less depressed at follow-ups up to six months later — long after the exercise itself ended. It was the strongest of the five interventions tested.
Step 3 of 6 · Friction

What actually got in the way?

One obstacle, named precisely. Then a rule for next time. This is the single mechanism that turns a bad day into a better system instead of just a worse mood.

Step 4 of 6 · Recovery

Say the thing you'd say to a friend.

If today went badly, self-criticism feels productive and isn't. In controlled experiments, people given a brief self-compassion prompt after a failure studied longer for a make-up test than those given a self-esteem boost or no prompt — because shame makes you avoid the thing.

Breines & Chen (2012), Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin 38(9):1133–1143. Four experiments: self-compassion after a failure increased self-improvement motivation relative to self-esteem boosts and controls.
Step 5 of 6 · Tomorrow

Decide the first move now.

Morning-you is not a reliable planner. Tonight-you already knows where you stopped. Make the first action so specific and so small that there is nothing left to decide at 8am.

Leave the runway lit. Physically stage the first action before you go to bed — the open book, the laid-out clothes, the tab left open. It removes the decision entirely rather than making it easier.
Step 6 of 6 · Wind-down

Land the plane properly.

Everything you studied today gets consolidated tonight or not at all. Treat sleep as the last step of studying, not the thing that comes after it.
