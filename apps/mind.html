The trial

Why cyclic sighing is the default

108 adults were randomised to five minutes daily of one of three breathwork protocols or mindfulness meditation, for a month. All four improved mood and lowered anxiety — but cyclic sighing (a double inhale through the nose, then a long slow exhale through the mouth) produced the largest improvement in daily positive affect and the biggest reduction in respiratory rate.

Balban, Neri, Kogon, Weed, Nouriani, Jo, Holl, Zeitzer, Spiegel & Huberman (2023), Cell Reports Medicine 4(1):100895.
The mechanism is the extended exhale. Exhalation lengthens the interval between heartbeats via vagal influence, which is why the exhale here is always longer than the inhale.
