01 · Before takeoff

Preflight

02 · Wheels up

Takeoff

The first four minutes are the only hard part. These rules exist to get you through them.

Procrastination is mood repair, not laziness. The urge to reorganise your notes is your brain trading a small amount of future pain for immediate relief. Naming it is usually enough to break it.
Sirois & Pychyl (2013), Social and Personality Psychology Compass 7(2):115–127.
03 · Stay airborne

Cruise

Sections are the unit of accomplishment. Timers are the unit of focus. Don't confuse the two.

Closing the book and retrieving beats rereading — by a lot. Students who studied a passage once then practised recall outperformed students who reread it four times, on a test a week later.
Roediger & Karpicke (2006), Psychological Science 17(3):249–255. · Karpicke & Blunt (2011), Science 331(6018):772–775 — retrieval practice also beat elaborative concept mapping.
